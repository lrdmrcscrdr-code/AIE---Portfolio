My story

I'm Lourd Marcus T. Cordero, a first-year AI Engineering student at Mapúa University. I'm at the beginning of my engineering journey, and I'm looking forward to developing my knowledge, technical skills, and perspective on how AI can be applied in meaningful ways.

I chose AI Engineering because it connects with my long-term career goal: to become a responsible user of AI who can simplify tasks and help solve real-world problems through intelligent technology. For me, learning AI isn't just about using advanced tools—it's also about understanding their limitations and using them thoughtfully.

As I continue my studies, I hope to build projects that show my progress, creativity, and ability to learn from challenges.

Academic laboratory projects

CM011L · EXPERIMENT 01 · GROUP REPORT

Basic Laboratory Techniques and Operations

Explored measurement of mass, length, volume, density, and temperature, as well as Bunsen burner operation. Our group compared heating methods and investigated how measurement and recording errors influence experimental results.

CM011L · EXPERIMENT 02 · GROUP REPORT

Determination of Acetic Acid in Vinegar

Applied acid–base titration and stoichiometric calculations to estimate the acetic acid content of a commercial vinegar sample. Our three trials produced an average of 2.110% by mass, below the 4.5% value on the product label; our report discusses potential experimental sources of error.
