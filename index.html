REFLECTION 01

Why I Chose AI Engineering

My interest in Artificial Intelligence started when I noticed how easily AI could make things entertaining online. Seeing what it was capable of made me wonder: if AI can do all these creative and entertaining things, why not use that same technology to create something that actually matters?

Read full reflection ↓

I've always been passionate about programming, and I originally considered pursuing Computer Science. However, when I discovered that Mapúa University was offering a degree in Artificial Intelligence Engineering, I saw an opportunity that I didn't want to miss. The idea of studying a field that focuses specifically on AI excited me, especially knowing how much potential this technology has for the future.

One of my biggest goals is to develop applications that can make everyday tasks easier. I want to explore how AI can automate repetitive processes, solve practical problems, and make technology more accessible to people who might not otherwise have the opportunity to use it.

At the same time, I believe that using AI responsibly is just as important as knowing how to use it. For me, that means being honest and transparent about when and how I use AI. For example, if I use AI to improve my grammar, I believe that should be acknowledged. When it comes to schoolwork, I see AI as a tool that can help me understand difficult concepts rather than something that should complete my assignments for me.

Ultimately, I chose AI Engineering because I want to be more than someone who simply uses AI. I want to understand how it works, build meaningful solutions with it, and make sure I'm using it in ways that genuinely benefit others.

REFLECTION 02

What I Hope to Learn

As I continue my journey in AI Engineering at Mapúa University, there's still so much I want to discover and understand. Although I've always been interested in programming and technology, I know that Artificial Intelligence is a much bigger field than simply knowing how to write code.

Read full reflection ↓

One of the things I'm most excited to learn is how AI actually works behind the scenes. I've seen what AI tools are capable of, but I want to understand the programming, mathematics, and logic that make these technologies possible. I'm particularly interested in learning how to build and train AI models from scratch, rather than only relying on existing tools.

At the same time, I also want to explore how existing AI technologies can be integrated into applications. I believe there's a lot of potential in combining AI with programming to automate repetitive tasks, simplify everyday activities, and make technology more accessible to others. Being able to both understand how AI models work and apply them to practical situations is something I hope to achieve throughout my studies.

Of course, I know that learning these skills won't always be easy. There will be complicated lessons, unfamiliar concepts, and projects that challenge my understanding. However, I see these challenges as opportunities to improve my problem-solving skills and become more confident in my abilities.

Most importantly, I want to learn how to use AI responsibly. I believe that understanding the limitations of AI is just as important as understanding its capabilities, especially when developing systems that other people may eventually rely on.

By the time I graduate, I hope to become someone who can both develop AI technologies and turn them into meaningful, practical solutions. More than anything, I want to use what I've learned to create something that genuinely makes people's lives easier.

REFLECTION 03

Growth Beyond the Classroom

Starting college at Mapúa University has been a big adjustment for me. As a first-year AI Engineering student, I've experienced challenges that have pushed me to grow, not just academically but also as a person.

Read full reflection ↓

I've come to realize that college isn't only about passing exams and completing requirements. It's also about learning how to manage myself, overcome difficulties, and work better with others.

One of my biggest challenges has been time management. With multiple subjects, assignments, and deadlines to keep track of, I've learned how important it is to stay organized and be responsible for my own work. I'm still figuring out what works best for me, but I've definitely become more aware of how I use my time.

Another important lesson has been learning how to deal with difficult problems. There have been times when certain lessons or activities felt overwhelming, especially when I couldn't understand something right away. Instead of giving up, I've learned to be more patient with myself, try different approaches, and accept that making mistakes is part of learning.

I've also learned a lot about teamwork and communication. Working on group activities and laboratory reports has helped me understand how important it is to contribute, listen to others, and communicate ideas clearly. I've realized that being a good team member isn't just about finishing your assigned tasks but also about making sure everyone is working toward the same goal.

Looking back, I can say that I've already grown in ways I didn't expect. I still have a lot to learn, especially when it comes to the technical side of AI Engineering, but I'm glad to see the progress I've made so far.

For me, college isn't just about becoming a better student. It's also about becoming someone who can handle challenges, work well with others, and continuously improve.
